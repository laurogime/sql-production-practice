## Assignment 2

You are a Data Engineer at an Australian fintech company.

The finance team has submitted the following request:

> *"We need to understand our loan portfolio risk exposure. For each loan product type, show us the total outstanding loan balance, the number of active loans, the average interest rate, and the average credit score of the borrowing customers. We only want active loans where the associated customer account is also active. Flag any loan product type where the average credit score of borrowers falls below 500 as 'High Risk', between 500 and 700 as 'Medium Risk', and 700 and above as 'Low Risk'."*

The relevant tables are `dbo.loans`, `dbo.accounts`, and `dbo.customers`.

You have 3 attempts. Submit your query when ready.
